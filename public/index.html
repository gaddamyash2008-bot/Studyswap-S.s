Frequently asked questions

Everything students need to know

How do I list a book?

Register as a seller, upload the book details, add condition and expected price, and share images.

Do I need to share my exact address?

No. Sellers can display city, locality and PIN code without revealing home addresses.

Can I request an unavailable book?

Yes. Buyers can submit book requests and sellers can match them with future supply.

Is the pricing result guaranteed?

No. It is a transparent recommendation based on known depreciation and comparable exchange evidence.
